What did you just learn?

Paste an explanation from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, or from your notes or a textbook paragraph. Read it carefully, because it's about to disappear.

Now teach it back, from memory.

Explain it as if you were teaching a 12-year-old. Use full sentences and plain words. No peeking (if you peek, Teachback will count it).
